What a water heater installation includes

Installation covers more than setting a tank on the floor. The water supply, a shutoff valve, the relief valve and its discharge pipe, a drain pan where the floor needs one, the fuel line or electrical circuit and the venting all have to be right.

For a gas heater we confirm the gas line can feed the unit and that combustion air and the flue work for the space. For an electric unit we confirm the breaker size and wire gauge match the label. Where a panel upgrade or a new gas run is needed, we say so at the start.

Placement also decides whether a tankless unit's venting and gas sizing are workable.

Permits, inspections and licensed work

Minnesota's plumbing code is Minnesota Rules chapter 4714, which is based on the 2018 Uniform Plumbing Code with state amendments. The City of South St. Paul lists water heaters among the residential permits available through its online permit portal.

State law lets owners do plumbing in their own home unless a local ordinance forbids it. If you hire out the work, verify the license through the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry lookup. A properly permitted install also keeps your records clean when you sell.

Sizing for a family, not a catalog

A tank sized for one adult runs out during a family morning. A tank oversized for two people wastes fuel all day. We ask how many people shower, whether you run a soaking tub and how often the dishwasher and washer run at once.
