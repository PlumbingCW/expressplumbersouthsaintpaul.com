Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website or call our line, and what we do with it.

24/7 emergency line
(855) 575-2890
Call now

What we collect

This website has no forms and no accounts. We do not ask you to enter personal details on any page. If you call (855) 575-2890, we collect the information you give us on the call, such as your name, address and a description of the problem. If you email contact@expressplumbersouthsaintpaul.com, we receive your email address and your message.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call after you hear that notice, you agree to the recording.

How we use information

We use what you tell us to book a visit, give you a price, perform the work and follow up. We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the plumbers and staff who need it to do the work, and with service providers who help us run the business.

Website data

Like most sites, our hosting provider may log technical data such as IP addresses, browser type and pages viewed. The site embeds Google Maps. When a map loads, Google may collect data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@expressplumbersouthsaintpaul.com. We will respond as the law requires.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom of the page shows the latest version.
