Who we serve and what we handle

Commercial work covers restaurants, retail shops, small offices, light industrial space and multifamily buildings with several units on one service. The fixtures are often the same as at home, but there are more of them, and downtime costs more.

We handle clogged mains, stoppages in restrooms, leaking supply lines, water heater failures, floor drains, faucets and flush valves. For larger projects such as a restroom build-out, we coordinate permits and scheduling around your hours.

Problems we see most in business buildings

These show up again and again in calls from owners and managers.

Restaurant kitchen backups

Grease builds in kitchen lines and traps. Regular cleaning and hydro jetting keep service from stopping.

Multifamily leaks

A tenant reports a ceiling stain, and the source is a unit above. Isolating units is the first step.

Restroom flush valves

High-use restrooms wear out flush valves and stall partitions faster than home toilets.

Backflow assemblies

Irrigation and fire systems often need an annual backflow test by a DLI-certified tester.

Water heater capacity

Salons, gyms and kitchens outgrow a residential tank. See replacement options.

Local notes for South St. Paul businesses

Concord Street, which is also state Highway 156, runs through South St. Paul, and our own office is in the Concord Exchange area. The city dates to 1887, the year the Stockyards Exchange Building went up, and buildings from that era and the decades after often have cast iron drains and galvanized supply lines.

If your building is old, ask for a camera look at the sewer before a tenant moves in. Sewer inspection gives a landlord a baseline. Large buildings and newer tenant improvements need permits, and the city's Building Permits and Inspections office issues them.

Do this first

A pipe is gushing in your shop

Business losses compound quickly when water reaches stock and electrical.

  • Close the building main or the nearest zone valve.
  • Cut power to affected outlets only if you can reach the panel safely.
  • Move stock, boxes and paper records off the floor.
  • Call the 24/7 line for same-day or after-hours dispatch and send us the address and access instructions.
