City well water and what it does to plumbing

Inver Grove Heights draws from six wells in the Jordan aquifer. The city's water treatment plant does not soften the water, and the city reports hardness of 20 to 22 grains per gallon, or about 370 parts per million. That is harder than the water in neighboring South St. Paul.

Hard water scales water heaters, tankless units and fixtures. The city also posts radium compliance notices on its site and states that they are not an emergency. If you want a softener, see water softener and filtration, and test your tap before you buy.

Septic or city sewer

About 60 percent of the city is on city sewer, and the rural remainder uses septic. If you are on city sewer, utility maintenance answers at 651-450-4309. If you are on septic, call a licensed septic contractor for the tank, and call us for the plumbing inside the house.

City code 8-4-7 prohibits connecting a sump pump to the sanitary sewer. If your pump is tied in, see sump pump service to reroute it outdoors. Fewer than 20 percent of homes here predate 1970, so many drain problems come from roots or grease rather than failed old pipe.
