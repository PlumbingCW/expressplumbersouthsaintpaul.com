The clear water rule

Mendota Heights Ordinance 496 bans clear water discharge into the sanitary sewer, and the city adds a quarterly surcharge for violations. A citywide sump pump inspection program ran in 2016 to find illegal connections.

If your pump, a floor drain or a downspout feeds the sanitary line, you can be paying the surcharge. We reroute discharges outdoors. See sump pump for failed pumps and basement waterproofing for groundwater.

The most frequent calls from the area are sump pumps that stop in spring, water heaters that fail and sewer service lines clogged by roots. Because the owner is responsible for the sewer service to the main, a main-line clog is the owner's repair.
