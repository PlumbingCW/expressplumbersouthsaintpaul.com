Water, sewer and who answers the phone

West St. Paul buys its drinking water from St. Paul Regional Water Services. The source is the Mississippi River, treated at the Maplewood plant, with chloramine for disinfection and fluoride added. SPRWS printed a hardness of 107 parts per million, about 6.25 grains per gallon, in its November 2025 mineral report. Test your tap, because monthly numbers move.

The city owns the sewer main and residents own the lateral. A backup that hits the whole block goes to the city sewer backup line at 651-552-4161. Domestic water plumbing permits are issued and inspected by SPRWS at 651-266-6270. We help you sort out which calls to make, then fix the part that is yours.
