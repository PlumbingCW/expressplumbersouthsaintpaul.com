Water Service Line Repair in South Saint Paul, MN

The water service line runs from the city main in the street, past the meter, to your house. Call (855) 575-2890 if you see a wet yard, lose pressure or hear that the city found a lead or galvanized line on your property.

The city's lead service line program

The City of South St. Paul reports that, as of October 17, 2025, its inventory counted about 740 lead service lines and about 175 galvanized lines that require replacement. The city says private-side replacement may be done at no cost to the owner with a signed agreement.

Before you pay for a replacement, call the city to ask whether your address is on the inventory and whether you qualify. We can handle the work if the program does not apply, or coordinate with the city's contractor if it does.

Signs of a service line problem

A buried leak rarely shows up as a gusher. Look for a soft or unusually green patch in the yard, a drop in pressure at every fixture, a meter that moves with all fixtures off or a rise in your usage on the bill.

A meter test helps. If the meter spins when everything inside is closed, the leak is on the service line or inside the house. Leak detection sorts out which.

Depth, frost and planning the dig

Minnesota's plumbing code in chapter 4714 requires buried water piping to sit at least 12 inches below local frost depth. For Dakota County the state frost depth is 3.5 feet, so a service line trench is about 4.5 feet deep.

That depth is why this is not a weekend project. We locate utilities, plan the trench around trees and driveways and restore the surface. If the break happened in winter, ask us about burst pipe repair timing, because frozen ground slows the dig.
