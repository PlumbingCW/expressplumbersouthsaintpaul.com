Pipe burst: the first ten minutes

What you do before the plumber arrives decides how much damage you get.

  • Close the main shutoff, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house.
  • Open the lowest faucet and flush upstairs toilets to drain the lines.
  • Turn off the water heater and the electrical circuit to wet rooms if you can reach the panel safely.
  • Photograph the damage for your insurer, then call the 24/7 line for same-day or after-hours dispatch.

Why pipes burst in a Minnesota winter

Water expands as it freezes, and the ice plug in a pipe pushes pressure against the section between the plug and the closed faucet. The pipe splits there, often a long lengthwise crack. You may not see the leak until the ice thaws and water flows.

Frozen pipes cluster in exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces, garages and under-sink cabinets on outside walls. Minnesota's plumbing code, chapter 4714, requires buried water piping to sit at least 12 inches below local frost depth, and the state sets frost depth at 3.5 feet for Dakota County.

Frozen versus burst: what to do

A frozen pipe is not yet a burst. Timing matters.

SituationWhat it meansYour move
No water at one faucet in cold weatherLikely frozen, not yet splitOpen the tap, apply gentle heat, call if no flow
Water stains or dripping after a thawProbably splitShut the main and call
Hissing in the wallActive leakShut the main and call
Ice on the pipe but flowingPartial freezeWarm the area and watch

Never use an open flame to thaw a pipe. A hair dryer or warm towels are safer.

What the repair looks like

We cut out the split section, install new pipe and fittings, and pressure test the line. If a long run has frozen and split in several places, we discuss whether to replace the run, and repiping is the option for homes with many failures.

For frozen service lines or a break between the meter and the house, see water service line repair. The City of South St. Paul says the owner is responsible for the service line from the building to the main.
