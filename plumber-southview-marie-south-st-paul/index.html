Where this area is

A 2014 Star Tribune report on the city's downtown planning study described a Southview and Marie area roughly bounded by Marie Avenue, Southview Boulevard, 7th Avenue and 12th Avenue. The report called it a mix of homes and small businesses, and said the planning study looked at how residential and commercial uses fit together.

That article also noted that residents liked how walkable the area is, and it listed "the Hilltop" among names people floated for it. We use the street names because they are the clearest way to describe where you are. If you are unsure whether your address is inside the area, call and give us the street. We will tell you plainly whether we can come.

What is verified, general, and unknown

Verified: the area is described as mixed residential and commercial, and South St. Paul was incorporated on March 2, 1887, so some buildings in town are very old.

General plumbing knowledge: in any city with older streets, homes and small shops built before the 1970s often have cast iron or clay drains and galvanized supply lines. Those pipes clog and corrode with age.

Unknown: we do not have a verified record of when each building on these blocks was built or what pipe it has. We will not guess. We check the pipe at your address.

Problems we are called about in mixed homes and shops

These are common plumbing calls in older neighborhoods with homes, apartments and small storefronts on the same blocks. They are general, not a claim about any one building.

Slow or repeating drains

A drain that backs up again within weeks usually has a blockage deeper than the fixture. A cable or a camera look finds the cause. See drain cleaning.

Main line backups

When several fixtures back up together, the problem is usually the line leaving the building. Sewer line cleaning clears it, and a camera shows why it keeps happening.

Hidden leaks

A damp wall, a warm spot on the floor or a water bill that climbs with no change in use can point to a leak. Leak detection finds it without opening every wall.

Water heaters

A heater with rust at the base, a pooling puddle or no hot water needs a look soon. See water heater repair.

Shared stacks in apartments

A building with units over a shop often shares one drain stack. A backup in one unit can show up in another, so we look at the whole line.

Do this first

If water is coming out where it should not

Act first, then call us.

  • Shut the nearest fixture valve or the main shutoff.
  • Turn off the water heater if the leak is near it.
  • Move belongings and stock off the floor.
  • Call us with your address. For a burst pipe, see burst pipe repair.
