About the Concord Street corridor

Public highway records state that Highway 156 is also known as Concord Street. The city itself was incorporated on March 2, 1887, and its main historical industry was the Saint Paul Union Stockyards, which have been closed since 2008. Much of that land is being redeveloped.

For a plumbing customer, the practical point is that a corridor like this mixes old and newer buildings, and homes sit near businesses. We do not assume what is behind any one door. We ask the address and the use of the building, then plan the visit.

What is verified, general, and unknown

Verified: Concord Street is also Highway 156. South St. Paul was incorporated in 1887. The stockyards closed in 2008.

General plumbing knowledge: storefronts and restaurants have different drain needs than houses. Grease, floor drains and shared building lines cause repeat backups. Water heaters in older buildings are often undersized or past their service life.

Unknown: we do not have a verified building-age list for Concord Street, and we do not claim any flood or soil condition here. If a building has a history of problems, tell us. That helps more than a guess.
