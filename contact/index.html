When you call, tell us your address, what is happening and whether water is still running. If you can reach the main shutoff, close it first. For a gas smell, leave the house and call Xcel Energy at 1-800-895-2999 or 911.

Before you call

  • Know your street address and nearest cross street
  • Find your main water shutoff
  • Take photos of any damage
  • Note what was running when it started
  • Tell us about pets, gates or access limits
